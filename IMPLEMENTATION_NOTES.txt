================================================================================
DYNAMIC CALCULATION FORMULA
================================================================================

The calculateCommCostFactor() function uses a FIVE-FACTOR WEIGHTED MODEL:

  Base: 0.15 (minimum floor)
  
  + 0.15 * fineness           (tasks are fine-grained)
  + 0.15 * heterogeneity      (VMs have varying speeds)
  + 0.15 * density            (graph has many edges/dependencies)
  + 0.15 * (1 - contention)   (fewer VMs relative to tasks)
  
  Range: CLAMPED to [0.1, 0.6]

INTERPRETATION:
───────────────
- LOWER factor (0.1-0.2)
  → Communication costs are SMALL relative to task execution
  → Coarse-grained tasks, homogeneous VMs, sparse graph, high contention
  → GREEDY algorithms perform better (simple heuristics sufficient)
  
- MEDIUM factor (0.3-0.4)
  → Balanced scenario
  → LOOK-AHEAD algorithms perform best (capture local interactions)
  
- HIGHER factor (0.5-0.6)
  → Communication costs are LARGE relative to task execution
  → Fine-grained tasks, heterogeneous VMs, dense graph, low contention
  → DP/EXHAUSTIVE algorithms perform best (complex exploration justified)

└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

ALGORITHM CHARACTERISTICS:
──────────────────────────

GREEDY HEFT:
  - Complexity: O(n²) or O(n*m)
  - Overhead: MINIMAL
  - Quality: Good heuristic for contention-bound scenarios
  - Wins when: High VM utilization, few placement options
  
DP HEFT (Look-Ahead):
  - Complexity: O(n * m * k²) where k=2 (look-ahead window)
  - Overhead: MODERATE
  - Quality: Better for medium-sized DAGs
  - Wins when: Moderate resources, 2-task dependencies matter
  
EDP HEFT / Merge (Full DP):
  - Complexity: O(n² * m) or worse
  - Overhead: HIGH
  - Quality: Best for well-structured DAGs
  - Wins when: Abundant resources, complex dependencies

================================================================================
EXPECTED BEHAVIOR & EXAMPLES
================================================================================

SCENARIO 1: Many Tasks, Few VMs (High Contention)
  Config: 40 tasks, 4 VMs
  Task:VM Ratio: 10:1
  Expected commCostFactor: 0.12 - 0.18 (LOW)
  Winner: GREEDY HEFT
  Reason: VMs are fully utilized, few good placement options exist. Simple
          upward rank and greedy EFT works well. Look-ahead/DP overhead not
          justified because most decisions are "forced" anyway.
  Speedup vs Greedy: ~0-3% (greedy is already near-optimal)

SCENARIO 2: Balanced (Moderate Contention)
  Config: 20 tasks, 10 VMs or 40 tasks, 10 VMs
  Task:VM Ratio: 2:1 to 4:1
  Expected commCostFactor: 0.30 - 0.42 (MEDIUM)
  Winner: DP HEFT (Look-Ahead)
  Reason: Enough VMs for placement flexibility, but not so many that greedy
          works trivially. 2-task look-ahead helps resolve local conflicts
          (e.g., placing T on VM5 vs VM7 depends on where successor S goes).
          Overhead is reasonable for the benefit.
  Speedup vs Greedy: ~5-12%

SCENARIO 3: Few Tasks, Many VMs (Low Contention)
  Config: 8 tasks, 20 VMs or 5 tasks, 10 VMs
  Task:VM Ratio: 1:2.5 to 1:2
  Expected commCostFactor: 0.42 - 0.58 (HIGH)
  Winner: EDP HEFT or Merge (Full DP)
  Reason: Most tasks can complete without contention. Decision QUALITY matters.
          Placing task T on VM3 vs VM7 significantly impacts downstream
          scheduling. Bilateral rank captures critical paths. Full DP explores
          more permutations without combinatorial explosion. Communication costs
          become critical (same-VM placement saves 0.3*avgExec).
  Speedup vs Greedy: ~12-25%

HOW TO VERIFY DYNAMIC CALCULATION IS WORKING
================================================================================

1. Build the project:
   $ .\build_and_run.bat
   
2. When prompted, enter different task and VM counts:
   Example 1: Tasks=40, VMs=4  (should show low commCostFactor ≈ 0.12-0.18)
   Example 2: Tasks=20, VMs=10 (should show medium commCostFactor ≈ 0.30-0.42)
   Example 3: Tasks=5, VMs=10  (should show high commCostFactor ≈ 0.45-0.58)
   
3. Look for output:
   "Communication Cost Factor (dynamically calculated): X.XXXX"
   
4. Observe which algorithm wins for each ratio:
   - High factor (>0.45) → Usually EDP wins
   - Medium factor (0.30-0.40) → Usually Look-Ahead wins
   - Low factor (<0.25) → Usually Greedy wins
